### Session description — under 1,500 characters
AI adoption often begins with the wrong question: “Which tool should we use?” A better starting point is: “How should the work actually flow?” Draw It First is a practical, tool-agnostic framework for mapping AI-assisted work before selecting software or automating steps. Using real examples from a working media operation, Mike Hamilton shows how to identify inputs, decisions, handoffs, quality controls, approval gates and the moments where human judgment still matters. Attendees will map one real workflow, decide what should remain human-led, identify safe automation opportunities, and define evidence that the new process is actually better. The goal is not more AI. It is better-designed work: fewer handoff failures, less rework, clearer accountability and measurable cycle-time improvement. The session is non-commercial and focuses on practical implementation, change management and performance improvement rather than promoting any vendor or product.

### Learning objectives
1. Map an AI-assisted workflow before selecting or deploying tools.
2. Distinguish human-judgment steps from safe automation opportunities.
3. Design approval gates, quality checks, handoffs and failure handling.
4. Define measurable evidence that the redesigned workflow is actually better.

### Intended audience
L&D leaders, training managers, workflow/process owners and managers responsible for AI adoption, workforce enablement or redesigning work around AI.

### Audience engagement
Participants map one real workflow during the session, marking inputs, decisions, human-only steps, AI-assisted steps, safe automation points and approval gates. They leave with a first-draft workflow they can continue refining with their team.

### Recommended categories
- Artificial Intelligence
- Strategy, Alignment and Planning
- Performance Management
