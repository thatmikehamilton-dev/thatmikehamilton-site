AI Content Workflow Audit

Before you buy another AI tool or automate a broken process, map the work first. In one focused engagement, we'll take a real content or media workflow apart, decide what should stay human, identify the right automation points, and turn it into a practical implementation plan.

  • 90-minute working session. We map the current process, inputs, decisions, handoffs, approval gates and failure points.
  • Draw It First workflow map. A clear view of what humans own, what AI can assist with, and what can safely be automated.
  • Tool and model recommendations. Use what you already have where it makes sense; add tools only where they solve a real bottleneck.
  • Repurposing and publishing map. Identify where one piece of work can create more useful outputs without multiplying production overhead.
  • Implementation priorities. A short, ranked action plan so your team knows what to change first.

Starting at $2,500. Best for media companies, creators, marketing teams and lean businesses that already make content but want a cleaner, more scalable AI-assisted production system.
