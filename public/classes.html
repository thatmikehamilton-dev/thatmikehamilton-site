The catch, up front

What these classes won't do

  • They won't make AI always right. It will sometimes be confidently wrong. You'll learn how to tell.
  • They won't sell you software. Most people should start free. I'll tell you when a paid plan earns its keep and when it doesn't.
  • They won't replace your judgment. That's the part you already have, and it's the part AI is worst at.

Common questions

Before you sign up

Do I need to know anything about AI first?

No. The basics class assumes nothing beyond being able to use a web browser.

What should I bring?

A laptop or tablet, and one real task you'd like help with: a letter, a plan, a confusing document, a repetitive chore at work.

How big are the classes?

Small on purpose, so everyone gets hands-on help.

Will there be a recording?

Ask when you reach out. The point is doing it yourself, live, with help in the room.
