Common questions

The short answers

Who is Mike Hamilton?

Mike Hamilton is a Waco, Texas–based media entrepreneur, AI speaker, and creator-technology builder. He's the founder of Rogue Media Network (200+ shows across all genres) and host of Seasoned.Tech, where he explains AI and creator tools honestly — for people who remember dial-up.

What does “AI and tech for people who remember dial-up” mean?

It means practical, no-hype AI — explained in plain English for adults who didn't grow up with a smartphone in hand. Real tools, real tradeoffs, and always the catch, so you can decide for yourself.

How often does the newsletter go out, and is it free?

Every weekday morning, and yes — it's free. Field notes on AI, tools, and the creator business, written plain and sent to your inbox. Unsubscribe anytime.

Can I hire Mike to speak or consult?

Yes. Mike speaks to experienced professionals, businesses, and organizations about using AI without surrendering human judgment, and consults on building real AI workflows. See Speaking and Consulting, or use the contact form below.
